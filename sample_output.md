# 🎟️ 8. Step‑By‑Step Booking & Execution Guide
1. **Book Train**  
   - Visit IRCTC or a trusted app (e.g., MakeMyTrip).  
   - Search “Gorakhpur → Jaipur” for the chosen date.  
   - Reserve a Sleeper (₹2,500) or 2AC (₹3,500).  
   - Print or save the e‑ticket; keep a backup copy.

2. **Reserve Accommodation**  
   - Go to Booking.com or Hostelworld.  
   - Filter for “budget” and “hostel” near City Palace.  
   - Book **Hoztel Jaipur** (₹1,800/night) + **Zostel Jaipur** (₹500/night) for the second night.  
   - Confirm cancellation policy (free cancellation until 24 h before check‑in).

3. **Plan Local Transport**  
   - Download Ola/Uber for auto rides; set a budget of ₹1,500.  
   - If renting a scooter, book via local rental app (e.g., Ather, Bounce) for ₹200/day.

4. **Purchase Entry Tickets**  
   - For **Amber Fort** and **Nahargarh Fort**, buy tickets online via the official portal or at the gate (₹150 each).  
   - For **Jantar Mantar** and **Albert Hall Museum**, entry is free or ₹30.

5. **Pack Essentials**  
   - Follow the packing checklist above.  
   - Include a small travel umbrella and a reusable water bottle.

6. **Day‑of‑Travel**  
   - Arrive at Gorakhpur station 2 hrs before departure.  
   - Keep your e‑ticket, ID proof, and a small amount of cash for local fares.  
   - On arrival in Jaipur, head straight to the hostel to drop luggage and start the itinerary.

7. **During the Trip**  
   - Use a local SIM (e.g., Airtel 4G) for navigation and communication.  
   - Keep a running expense log to stay within the ₹15,000 budget.

8. **Return**  
   - Confirm train departure time.  
   - Leave the hostel 1 hr before departure to avoid last‑minute rush.  

Enjoy your budget‑friendly heritage adventure from Gorakhpur to Jaipur!
